Measured data — validation, suitability and anomaly check

ML module status — proposal vs page (reconciled). The FYP proposal commits to two ML modules: (1) Random Forest for site suitability and power prediction, (2) Isolation Forest for anomaly detection. Status today: neither is trained. The suitability score below is a transparent rule score (baseline, /100) and the anomaly check is a simple median/MAD check on uploaded measured data only. A Random Forest trained on 21 sites with placeholder heads and no measured power would be statistically weak — it will only be trained after field data arrives (measured head and power, 5 Dec 2026 plan), with cross-validation, feature importance and honest limits reported. The Isolation Forest will only run on a measured operating history. Until then, no trained model is claimed anywhere on this page.

Economics — selected reach

Type a value — every number below updates at once. Costs are planning estimates by turbine type and size (power law: cost per kW rises as the plant shrinks) — confirm with supplier quotes. Formulas: LCOE = (capital × CRF + annual O&M) ÷ annual kWh, where CRF = r(1+r)ⁿ ÷ ((1+r)ⁿ − 1). NPV = −capital + Σ annual net cash ÷ (1+r)ₛ. IRR is the discount rate that makes NPV zero. Payback = capital ÷ (annual value − annual O&M).

Sources & assumptions: the tariff values used here — 22 PKR/kWh (sale to grid) and 38 PKR/kWh (self-consumption) — and the 10% discount rate are planning assumptions, not quotations from a NEPRA tariff determination or the State Bank of Pakistan policy rate. Confirm the current tariff and discount rate before citing them in the report.

How this is calculated

Method in brief — how the numbers are calculated

Power at one operating point: P = 9.81 × Q × H net × η turbine(Q) × η generator. H net = gross head at the fall − losses (trash rack + intake + friction + exit). Annual energy: monthly flow factors × (days in month − closure days) × 24 × 0.95 availability, with flow capped at the turbine design flow. Design flow: Q50 / Q60 / Q70 of the monthly flow-duration curve, or full canal discharge. Economics: capital = rated kW × cost per kW, where cost per kW = base × (rated ÷ 100)−exponent for the turbine family (planning estimate). LCOE = (capital × CRF + O&M) ÷ annual kWh. NPV and IRR use the annual net cash flow over the project life at the discount rate.

Limitations — read before you cite a number

  • Fall heads are placeholders (2.5 m) — no measured head per fall yet. Ranking follows head × flow, so it changes when real heads arrive.
  • No gauge data yet — monthly factors and closure days are planning estimates per canal.
  • Costs are planning estimates by turbine type and size — not quotations. Confirm with supplier quotes.
  • No trained machine-learning model — suitability is a transparent rule score and the anomaly check is a simple median/MAD check, both active only with uploaded measured data.
  • Kabul River Main Canal (kr1): the stored “length” 1,500 is not a length — it is the offtake RD (1,500 ft). CCA is missing. kr1 is excluded from the Top 5 until length and CCA are verified against the progress report.
  • Lengths in this tool are in feet, not metres (Lower Swat Main 148,000 ft = 45.1 km; Kabul River Canal 198,000 ft = 60.4 km). Lengths are shown in km with the ft value beside them.
  • ML status: no trained Random Forest or Isolation Forest yet — see the ML module status panel. The proposal's ML modules will only be trained on measured data, with cross-validation.
  • Totals across reaches are not shown — branches reuse the same water, so reaches are not additive. Use the Top 5 list instead.
  • Longitudinal profile (in the downloadable report) is schematic (datum 100, not surveyed) — real geometry needs a QGIS GeoJSON export.
